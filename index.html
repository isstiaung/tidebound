Tidebound

Every card carries two numbers: one for the sun, one for the moon. Three shrines, two skies, and a tide that keeps turning them over. Take two shrines to win.

The Voyage

    Chronicle

      Pass the device over. Your hand stays hidden until you're ready.

      How to play

      1. Two numbers, one counts. Each card has a sun number and a moon number. In a shrine, a card counts the number that matches the shrine's sky, and the card turns so that number is on top.
      2. Play one card a turn. Drag it, or tap it and then a shrine, to put it on your side (four places each). Then you draw.
      3. The tide turns. After every round, the tide flips the sky of the marked shrine, then moves on to the next one. You always know where it goes next.
      4. Shrines seal. When both sides of a shrine are full, it seals under its current sky. The higher total takes it. A tie goes to whoever filled their side first.
      5. Take two shrines to win. If the shrines end up split evenly, the higher total across all shrines wins. If neither player can move, every open shrine seals as it stands.

      Plain cards always add up to 10, so a card that shines under the sun is weak under the moon. Whoever moves first starts with an extra card, since moving second lets you see more before you commit.

      Special cards

      Keyboard

      Number keys pick a card from your hand. Q, W and E play it to the left, middle or right shrine. Esc cancels.
